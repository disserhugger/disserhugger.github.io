How To Play

Bayats are scattered across the arena and they do not want to be hugged. Chase them down, get close, and hug them before time runs out.

MoveWASD or Arrow Keys
HugJust get close enough
PauseEsc or P
ComboHug fast, chain hugs

Arcade Mode

You get 60 seconds. Hug as many Bayats as possible. You automatically get stronger as you level up — no choices, just chaos.

Full Game Mode

Instead of health, you have a countdown timer. Hugging Bayats adds time back — but the bonus shrinks the longer you survive, so you'll always need the next hug. Level up to choose upgrades and unlock tools.

Bayat Types

  • Normal — balanced.
  • Fast » — hard to catch, worth more.
  • Slow ~ — easy, worth less.
  • Tiny · — small hitbox, unpredictable.
  • Giant !! — slow but a big reward.
  • Dangerous ☠ (red) — do not hug this one, it costs you time.
  • Golden ★ — rare, extremely fast, huge reward. Chase it down!
  • Boost ⚡ — hug it for a random temporary buff (speed, reach, EXP, or cooldowns).
  • Snowball ❄ — throws snowballs from range that slow you down. Dodge them!
  • Bomb — charges at you and arms itself when close. Hug it before it detonates, or back off to defuse the countdown.
